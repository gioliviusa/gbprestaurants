How to Get More Google Business Profile Views & Calls

Published: January 15, 2026 | 5 min read

Your Google Business Profile is one of the most valuable tools for driving customers to your restaurant. But just having a profile isn't enough—you need to actively optimize it to maximize views and phone calls.

1. Optimize Your Business Information

Start with the basics. Ensure your restaurant name, address, phone number, and hours are accurate and complete. Google rewards complete profiles with higher visibility.

  • Business Name: Include your restaurant name exactly as you want it to appear
  • Contact Phone: Use the main number customers call
  • Hours: Update for holidays and special hours
  • Service Areas: Specify all neighborhoods and regions you serve

2. Post Weekly to Your Profile

Google Business Posts are the most effective way to increase visibility. Posts appear directly in search results and Google Maps, giving potential customers immediate reasons to visit or call.

Pro Tip: Post at least 1-2 times per week featuring new menu items, specials, events, and behind-the-scenes content. This signals active management to Google and increases your profile's prominence.

3. Add High-Quality Photos & Videos

Restaurants with professional photos get 40% more clicks to their website and 35% more direction requests. Add photos of:

  • Your restaurant exterior and interior
  • Signature dishes and menu highlights
  • Your team and restaurant atmosphere
  • Events and special promotions

4. Respond to All Reviews (Positive & Negative)

Review responses increase customer trust and show Google that you actively manage your profile. Aim to respond within 24-48 hours.

  • Positive Reviews: Thank customers and invite them back
  • Negative Reviews: Respond professionally and offer to resolve the issue

5. Use Google Posts with CTAs

Google Posts allow you to add call-to-action buttons like "Order Online," "Call," "Book Now." Use these strategically to convert profile views into actual phone calls and reservations.

6. Encourage Customer Reviews

Restaurants with more recent reviews rank higher. Ask customers for reviews via:

  • QR codes at tables linking to your Google review page
  • Email follow-ups after dining
  • Text messages with review links
  • Social media mentions

Results You Can Expect

Restaurants that implement these strategies typically see:

  • +50-80% increase in profile views within 30 days
  • +40-60% increase in phone calls and direction requests
  • +20-30% increase in reservations
