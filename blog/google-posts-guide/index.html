The Complete Guide to Restaurant Google Posts

Published: January 18, 2026 | 7 min read

Google Posts are the most effective way to keep your restaurant visible in Google Search and Google Maps. This guide covers everything you need to know to maximize their impact.

What Are Google Posts?

Google Posts are short, timely updates that appear directly in your restaurant's Google Business Profile. They show up in search results when customers search for your business or similar restaurants. Posts last 7 days (or until your promotion ends), making them perfect for time-sensitive announcements.

Types of Google Posts

  • Events: Promote special events, happy hours, or dinners
  • Offers: Share discounts, free items, or limited-time promotions
  • News & Updates: Announce new menu items, reopenings, or changes
  • Products: Highlight signature dishes or new offerings

Google Posts Best Practices

1. Post Consistently (2x Per Week)

Google rewards active profiles. Establish a posting schedule and stick to it. This signals to Google that your business is current and engaged.

2. Add High-Quality Images

Posts with images get 60% more engagement than text-only posts. Use:

  • Clear, appetizing photos of your dishes
  • Well-lit images (natural lighting preferred)
  • Your restaurant logo or branding

3. Use Strong Calls-to-Action

Every post should drive action. Use buttons like:

  • "Order Online"
  • "Call to Reserve"
  • "Learn More"
  • "Book Now"

4. Keep Text Concise

Write 100-150 characters maximum. Mobile users need to see your full message immediately. Example: "🍕 New Truffle Pizza available this week only! Order online or call 555-0123."

5. Use Relevant Keywords

Include keywords related to your offerings. For example:

  • "Happy Hour: $3 Appetizers & $5 Draft Beers 4-6pm"
  • "Gluten-Free Pasta Now Available"
  • "Live Jazz Friday & Saturday Nights"

Content Calendar for Restaurants

Monday-Tuesday: Weekly specials and happy hour announcements

Wednesday-Thursday: New menu items or featured dishes

Friday-Saturday: Weekend events and dinner specials

Sunday: Brunch announcements and week ahead preview

Google Posts + Reviews Strategy

Combine Google Posts with an active review strategy. Post consistently to show engagement, and ask customers for reviews in those posts. This creates a virtuous cycle: more posts → more visibility → more customers → more reviews → higher rankings.

Measuring Success

Google Business Profile Analytics show you:

  • How many people saw each post
  • How many clicked your post or CTA
  • Call and direction request trends

Track these metrics weekly to identify your best-performing posts and refine your strategy.
