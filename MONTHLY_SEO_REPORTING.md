# Monthly SEO Reporting Framework for GBP Restaurants

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the monthly SEO reporting process to track progress toward ranking goals and measure the ROI of SEO/GEO efforts.

**Reporting Date**: 1st of each month
**Data Collection**: Previous calendar month
**Report Format**: PDF + Dashboard + Team Meeting (30 min)
