What is Google Business Profile management for restaurants?

Google Business Profile management for restaurants is the ongoing work of keeping a restaurant’s free Google listing — the one shown in Google Search and Google Maps — accurate, active, and optimised. That includes publishing weekly Google Posts, uploading food and interior photos, keeping menu links and hours current, replying to reviews, monitoring Q&A, and tracking profile insights. GBP Restaurants provides this as a done-for-you monthly retainer starting at $297 per month, with no contracts.
